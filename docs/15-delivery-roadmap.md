# Delivery Roadmap — AliAuction.ca

The business plan describes three delivery phases: MVP in Months 1–4, enhancements in Months 5–8 and advanced features in Months 9–12. The roadmap below translates those phases into workstreams. Actual estimates depend on team size, requirements decisions, vendor onboarding and legal review.

## Phase 0 — Discovery and foundations

**Suggested duration:** Before implementation / sprint zero

Deliverables:
- Stakeholder sign-off on PRD and MVP scope.
- Confirm auction rules and fee model.
- Obtain companion wireframes/design specifications.
- Confirm Canadian legal/privacy/payment review plan.
- Architecture decision record.
- Database ERD and migration plan.
- UX flows and design system.
- Development environments and CI pipeline.
- Threat model and data classification.

Exit criteria:
- Open decisions blocking core development are resolved.
- MVP backlog is estimated and prioritized.
- Team can deploy a basic application to a test environment.

## Phase 1 — MVP (business-plan Months 1–4)

Workstreams:
1. Identity and account management.
2. Seller profile and basic verification workflow.
3. Categories and listing management.
4. Timed auction engine.
5. Manual bidding and approved proxy-bidding baseline.
6. Search and browsing.
7. Checkout and selected payment provider.
8. Orders and basic fulfilment.
9. Admin moderation and audit trail.
10. Notifications.
11. Responsive web/PWA.
12. Security, privacy, support and observability.

Exit criteria:
- P0 PRD requirements accepted.
- Critical end-to-end journeys pass.
- Auction and payment tests pass.
- Policies and legal review complete.
- Production support and monitoring ready.

## Phase 2 — Enhancements (business-plan Months 5–8)

Deliverables:
- Native mobile apps (React Native).
- Seller analytics and reporting.
- Advanced automated bidding.
- Snipe protection and configurable bid increments.
- Watchlist and notification preferences.
- Seller reputation and badges.
- Carrier rate and shipping-label integration.

Exit criteria:
- Mobile apps pass platform release requirements.
- Carrier flows are reconciled and tested.
- Analytics definitions are documented.
- Reputation/badge criteria are transparent.

## Phase 3 — Advanced features (business-plan Months 9–12)

Deliverables:
- Live auction streaming and real-time bidding.
- AI recommendations.
- High-value escrow-like workflow after legal/provider approval.
- Third-party API marketplace.
- Image recognition and auto-categorization.
- Advanced fraud detection.

Exit criteria:
- Human review and appeals exist for AI-assisted risk decisions.
- Streaming and bidding are load-tested.
- Financial/legal controls for escrow-like service are approved.
- API security, quotas and developer terms are defined.

## Cross-phase workstreams

- Security and privacy.
- Compliance and legal.
- Product analytics.
- Customer support.
- Seller acquisition and onboarding.
- Incident response and reliability.
- Documentation and training.

## Team roles

The business plan projects:
- Year 1: 8 FTEs.
- Year 2: 15 FTEs.
- Year 3: 25 FTEs.

Its Year 1 split is 2 executive, 3 engineering, 1 marketing, 1 operations and 1 customer support. Validate whether this staffing is sufficient for product delivery, security, compliance, support coverage and operations.

## Planning caution

The source plan's 12-month feature roadmap and Month 16 breakeven target are projections. Treat delivery dates as planning assumptions until technical discovery, staffing, vendor onboarding and legal dependencies are validated.
